# Website analytics (Pirsch)

This website uses [Pirsch Analytics](https://pirsch.io) to count visits and understand which content is read. Pirsch is a privacy-friendly analytics service operated by Emvi Software GmbH in Germany, with data stored on servers in the European Union.

- **No cookies** are set and nothing is stored on your device for analytics.
- Your **IP address is not stored**. To count unique visitors, Pirsch creates a fingerprint by hashing your IP address, user agent, the date and a random salt. The salt changes every day, so visitors cannot be tracked across days or across websites.
- Data collected: the page visited, referrer, browser, operating system, device type, screen size, language and country (derived from the IP address, which is then discarded).
- The data is only used in aggregated form. It is not shared with third parties and not used for advertising or profiling.

**Legal basis:** legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR) in understanding how the website is used and improving its content. You can object to this processing at any time; blocking the script `api.pirsch.io` in your browser also prevents any analytics data from being sent.

More information: [Pirsch privacy policy](https://pirsch.io/privacy).

# Hosting (GitHub Pages)

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, operated by GitHub, Inc. (USA). When you visit the site, GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address for security and to keep the service running. GitHub participates in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. See the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) for details.

**Legal basis:** legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR) in securely and reliably delivering this website.

# Contact by e-mail

If you send an e-mail, your e-mail address and the content of your message are only used to answer your question. They are kept no longer than necessary for that purpose.

**Legal basis:** legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR) in replying to your message, or steps prior to entering into a contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).

# Local storage

If you switch between light and dark mode, your choice is saved in your browser's local storage (`bb-theme`). This value never leaves your device and is not used for tracking.

# External links

This website links to external websites such as GitHub, LinkedIn and YouTube. Once you follow such a link, the privacy policy of that website applies.

# Your rights

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Belgian Data Protection Authority ([www.dataprotectionauthority.be](https://www.dataprotectionauthority.be)).
